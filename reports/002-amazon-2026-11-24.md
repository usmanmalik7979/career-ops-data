Program Manager II
Amazon UK Services Ltd.
GB, London
https://www.amazon.jobs/en/jobs/10608243/program-manager-ii

DESCRIPTION
Amazon's Quick Commerce business is expanding across the EU, and we need a Program Manager II to help us launch new sites that bring faster delivery to more customers. In this role, you will own the full lifecycle of site launches — from supporting site selection through to handing over a location ready for operations. You will coordinate across many teams including but not limited to: real estate, design and construction, finance, operations, and technology teams to keep launches on time and on budget. Quick Commerce is still a new and rapidly developing business at Amazon, which means you will work in an environment where processes are being built and refined in real time. If you enjoy bringing structure to ambiguity, holding stakeholders accountable, and seeing a project through from start to finish, this is a role where your work will have direct, visible impact.

Key job responsibilities
- Own end-to-end program management for EU Quick Commerce site launches, defining milestones, managing schedules, and keeping delivery on track against timelines and budgets.
- Coordinate across stakeholders, ensuring alignment and driving decisions that keep launches moving forward.
- Identify risks early in the launch lifecycle, develop mitigation plans, and escalate when needed to prevent delays or scope issues.
- Develop and refine scalable launch processes, looking for opportunities to simplify workflows and increase efficiency as the program grows.
- Define and track program metrics and success criteria, using data to inform trade-offs and recommend improvements for future launches.

A day in the life
You might start your morning reviewing the status of active site launches and preparing an update for leadership. Mid-morning, you could be on a call with the design and construction team working through a timeline dependency, then pivot to a site visit. After lunch, you may visit a site in progress or join a working session with operations to confirm readiness requirements. Your afternoon could include refining the launch playbook based on lessons from a recent go-live, then wrapping up by aligning next steps with your fellow program managers.

About the team
Our team is a group of program managers dedicated to launching Quick Commerce sites across the EU. We own the full project lifecycle, working at the intersection all key stakeholders to bring new locations online. Quick Commerce is one of Amazon's newer and fastest-growing businesses, and we are expanding our infrastructure portfolio to reach more customers. If you want to be part of building something from the ground up within an inclusive, collaborative team, this is an exciting time to join.

BASIC QUALIFICATIONS
- Knowledge of Excel (Pivot Tables, VLookUps) at an advanced level and SQL
- Experience working cross functionally with tech and non-tech teams
- Experience defining program requirements and using data and metrics to determine improvements
- Experience in program or project management
- Experience implementing repeatable processes and driving automation or standardization
- Experience defining and executing program requirements
- Bachelor's degree

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS
- Experience in driving end to end delivery, and communicating results to senior leadership
- Experience leading process improvements
- Experience in stakeholder management, dealing with multiple stakeholders at varied levels of the organization
- Experience building processes, project management, and schedules
